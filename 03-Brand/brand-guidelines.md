## Executive Summary
i2Print already owns a strong visual idea it doesn't use in its words. The logo is a **stopwatch whose face is a color wheel**, with the tagline **"service. speed. success."** in cyan, yellow and magenta, the process ink colors. That idea says *fast, colorful, made by printers*. The website copy says something else: "Best value online printing company," a line national online printers commonly use.

The recommended voice `[proposed]` is **the neighborhood print expert who's fast and helps get your file right**. Customers should feel they're dealing with a real shop in Long Island City, with people who answer the phone and catch problems before they print, on a turnaround the owner confirms. National printers like Vistaprint can't make that claim. 4OVER4, which has a Queens hub with NYC pickup, is the closest rival and has to be out-served on personal attention rather than out-claimed.

**Current state `[verified]`:** template copy, lots of exclamation marks, typos in the hero banners, and no local proof: no shop photos, hours, map or pickup info. **Recommended evolution `[proposed]`:** short, confident, specific copy (times, sizes, pickup), real shop photography, and the stopwatch/color-wheel concept doing the visual work.

### Voice Attributes Detail
#### 1. Local
- **What it means:** every page reminds people there's a physical shop in Queens.
- **How it shows up:** address, map, hours and pickup in the header or footer; neighborhood names (LIC, Astoria, Sunnyside, Hunters Point); real photos of the shop.
- **Avoid:** "online printing company" as the main identity.
- **Evidence:** the existing tagline "Everyone's local printer" (homepage) and the published LIC address `[verified]`. competitors.md: among the competitors reviewed, "nobody combines real online ordering with a clearly local, human, fast story. 4OVER4 is the closest." Coe Displays' and Triboro's websites weren't reviewed.
- **Confidence:** Medium

#### 2. Fast
- **What it means:** speed is the brand. It's literally a stopwatch.
- **How it shows up:** turnaround badges ("Approve by noon → ready tomorrow"; **real times to be supplied by the client**), and "ready for pickup" notifications.
- **Avoid:** promising times the shop can't hit.
- **Evidence:** the logo stopwatch and the tagline "service. speed. success." (logo files) `[verified]`; the "Swift Turnaround" benefit heading on the homepage `[verified 2026-10-20]`. No actual turnaround times are published.
- **Confidence:** Low–Medium (the brand intent is clear; the operating facts aren't)

#### 3. Expert
- **What it means:** printers who understand bleed, DPI and color, and explain it simply.
- **How it shows up `[proposed]`:** a "Free file check" promise, a file-prep guide with real measurements, and the proposed file-fix tool.
- **Avoid:** jargon without explanation, or blaming the customer's file.
- **Evidence:** the FAQ explains 300 dpi and RGB→CMYK conversion, and PDF proofs are required before production `[verified]`, which shows some technical guidance exists. The gap: bleed and safe-zone sizes aren't published (brand-audit.md §4.6). It isn't known whether staff check files today.
- **Confidence:** Low. Whether to promise a free file check is the owner's call.

#### 4. Straightforward
- **What it means:** short sentences, prices visible, and a single exclamation mark at most.
- **How it shows up:** "Business cards from $16.67. 250 cards, ready tomorrow." *(the quantity and timing are placeholders)*
- **Avoid:** "Welcome to i2print inc." as a headline, and "Here's what our users speak about us."
- **Evidence:** this is a corrective attribute. Current copy does the opposite: frequent exclamation marks (brand-audit §3), and only 4 of about 20 products show a price (brand-audit §4.5). The national printers set the "from $X" expectation (competitors.md).
- **Confidence:** Low

#### 5. Colorful and precise
- **What it means:** the color wheel and CMY accents carry personality; the layouts and copy are exact and typo-free.
- **How it shows up:** CMY "ink bar" accents, clean grids, proofread banners.
- **Avoid:** typos, lorem ipsum, cluttered sale graphics.
- **Evidence:** the logo's color wheel and CMY tagline `[verified]`. The current banners contain "BUINESS", "busniess", "CHOSSE" and lorem ipsum (brand-audit §4.1) `[verified]`.
- **Confidence:** Medium

#### 6. Helpful to small businesses
- **What it means:** speak to local owners (restaurants, shops, contractors) with practical examples.
- **Evidence:** the product mix of business stationery, marketing materials, signs and car magnets (brand-audit §1) `[verified]`. The real customer mix is unknown.
- **Confidence:** Low. Confirm the customer segments in discovery.

## Messaging Framework *(Confidence: Low. Needs owner input)*

### Primary Value Proposition (draft)
> **Queens' fast local printer: files checked, prices up front, pickup in Long Island City.**

Variations seen on the current site:
- "Everyone's local printer" (homepage)
- "Best value online printing company. High quality print delivered!" (homepage)
- "Speed, Service, and Quality. Your Success is Our Commitment!" (homepage)
- "service. speed. success." (logo)

### Key Message Pillars
1. **Ready when you need it.** Turnaround and pickup times are specific and visible.
   - Use it on the homepage hero, product pages and email subject lines.
   - Example: "Order by noon. Pick up tomorrow on 24th St." *(times TBD)*
2. **We check every file `[proposed: depends on the owner adopting a free file check]`.** A human or tool review before anything prints.
   - Use it on upload steps, the file-prep page and in reviews.
   - Example: "Not sure your file's print-ready? We'll check it free and show you a proof first."
3. **Real shop, real people.** The address, team and presses.
   - Use it on the About page, footer, Google Business Profile and social.
   - Example: "Printing in Long Island City since [year]." *(founding year to confirm)*
4. **Fair prices you can see.** Starting prices on every product.
   - Use it on product grids and in promos.
   - Example: "Business cards from $16.67." (the published price)

### Competitive Positioning
- **vs. Vistaprint / MOO / national online printers:** same online convenience, plus local pickup, a human file check, and someone to call.
- **vs. 4OVER4 (Queens hub):** the independent neighborhood shop; personal service over a big catalog. *(Confirm i2Print's real same-day capability before claiming parity.)*
- **vs. local quote-only shops (GSB Digital, SpeedPro LIC):** online ordering already exists `[verified]`. The target state `[proposed]` is visible prices on every product, so customers aren't waiting on a quote.
- **vs. doing nothing:** the current site's typos and missing local signals may cost trust with first-time visitors.

## Tone-by-Context Matrix *(Confidence: Low. Extrapolated)*

| Context | Formality | Energy | Technical Depth | Key Principle |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Website homepage/product pages | Medium-Low | High | Low | Price, time, place: in that order |
| File-prep / upload guidance | Medium | Calm | Medium-High | Explain once, plainly; offer the fix |
| Promo / newsletter email | Low | High | Low | One offer, one deadline, one button |
| B2B / reseller quotes | Medium-High | Medium | High | Specs, turnaround, volume pricing |
| Social media | Low | High | Low | Show the work: presses, finished jobs, local customers *(with permission)* |
| Order/pickup notifications | Medium | Warm | Low | Clear status + what to do next |

### Context-Specific Guidelines
- **Homepage:**
  - Do: lead with the local and fast promise, show starting prices, use real photos.
  - Don't: "Welcome to…" headlines, carousels of stock mockups, ALL-CAPS sale banners.
- **File-prep:**
  - Do: publish exact bleed and safe-zone sizes per product, with diagrams.
  - Don't: say "file rejected" without showing the fix.
- **Email:**
  - Do: put the offer in the subject line ("50% off your first business cards").
  - Don't: send more than one promo a week, or use exclamation marks in subject lines.
- **Social:**
  - Do: post short videos of jobs coming off the press.
  - Don't: post client work without the client's permission.

## Open Questions for the Discovery Call

### High priority (blocks the guideline)
1. **Local-first or online-first?**
   - What was found: the site says both "Everyone's local printer" and "Best value online printing company."
   - Recommendation: local-first, with online convenience as the second message.
   - Need from you: the owner's view of where revenue really comes from (walk-in/local B2B vs. shipped online orders).
2. **What turnaround can we actually promise?**
   - What was found: "Swift Turnaround," with no times anywhere.
   - Recommendation: publish 2–3 honest tiers per product family (e.g., standard / rush).
   - Need from you: real production and cutoff times by product.
3. **Founding year.**
   - What was found: About says 2000 / 25 years (2026 − 2000 = 26, so that copy may be stale); third-party sources (atly.com, leadiq.com, both unverified) say 1990.
   - Recommendation: use whatever the owner confirms, and only that.
   - Need from you: the confirmed year.

4. **Walk-in pickup and store hours.** What was found: neither is published. Recommendation: publish both if pickup is offered; it's the core of the "local" attribute. Need from you: whether pickup is offered, and the hours.
5. **Does anyone check files today?** What was found: proofs are required, but there's no mention of a file review. Recommendation: describe what happens today accurately before promising more. Need from you: the current process and the reprint policy.

### Medium priority
6. **Same-day capability.** What was found: 4OVER4 advertises same-day NYC pickup. Recommendation: only claim it if the shop can reliably deliver it. Need from you: which products, and what cutoff.
7. **Is `NewI2!` a standing offer?** Recommendation: if yes, make it the newsletter signup incentive. Need from you: confirmation and an end date, if any.
8. **Platform.** What was found: almost certainly OnPrintShop (inferred from file paths). Need from you: confirmation, plan and contract term.
9. **Free file check?** Recommendation: yes, it's the strongest differentiator and it feeds the file-fix tool. Need: the owner's willingness and staff capacity.
10. **Which blue?** Recommendation: one accessible primary blue. Need: approval for the color adjustment.
11. **Photo shoot.** Recommendation: a half-day shoot before launch. Need: approval, plus permission to show the shop, staff and client work.
12. **Client logos.** What was found: 19 client logos are displayed on the current site. Recommendation: don't reuse any of them on the new site until written permission is confirmed; ask the owner which clients will agree. Need: the list of approved logos.

### Low priority
13. **Tagline.** Keep "service. speed. success." as the logo lockup, and use the value proposition line on the web. Need: confirmation.
14. **Address format.** The footer reads "3819, 24th Street"; this draft uses "38-19". Recommendation: match the Google Business Profile and USPS format. Need: confirmation.
