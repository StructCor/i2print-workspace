04 / DELIVERY APPROACH
Approve one usable milestone at a time
i2Print × RaxaRisk, LLC  /  Daniel Raxa  /  Private review copy
04
Proposed sequence. Each milestone ends with something your team can review. The transition
plan must be tested; uninterrupted ordering is a goal, not a guarantee.
1 — Discovery and design
Confirm audience, workflows, ownership expectations, and the first release. Review the sitemap and key
screens. Gate: agreed scope, requirements, and design direction.
2 — Website alongside current ordering
Build and test the approved website while existing ordering remains in place. Agree how the two
connect. Gate: accessible pages, correct content, working links, and an approved launch plan.
3 — Validate a commerce migration
Check actual exports and prototype the hardest pricing example before selecting a store platform. Move
one product family at a time only after validation. Gate: reconciled data, tested payments and order
handling, redirects, and a recovery plan. Timing is owner-pending.
4 — Introduce automation incrementally
Start with the most useful repeatable task identified in discovery. Test with sample data and staff review
before expanding. Gate: correct outputs, clear exception handling, and an accountable approver.
5 — Ongoing care
Agree responsibility for maintenance, monitoring, access, backups, support, and small improvements.
Review observed results before choosing the next investment.
