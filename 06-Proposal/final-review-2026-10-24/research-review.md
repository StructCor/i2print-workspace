# Research review and resulting decisions

Reviewed October 24, 2026. Both supplied PDFs are ten pages; extracted text and corresponding rendered pixels are identical. Both original attachments remain unchanged. Extracted copies and rendered contact sheets are retained in research/.

## Applied to the final proposal
- Preserve i2Print's identity: white background, logo, Jost and existing blue/orange/CMY tokens. Claude's original concept is the visual baseline.
- Explain capabilities through the customer's intended job, with accessible process choices and illustrative motion. The official About page publishes digital Indigo, offset, wide format and die-cut/packaging. It does not establish a current machine inventory, models, capacity, materials or availability.
- Preserve routes for business and personal/event printing. The report's recommendation to investigate repeat business is a hypothesis; no segment is described as the largest without order/revenue mix.
- Improve inquiry structure and artwork guidance. A limited local quote/image/proof demo illustrates the journey without creating a real order or production authorization.
- Audit current OnPrintShop licensing, configuration, usage, integrations and exports. The existing FAQ already advertises pricing, proof approval, saved designs and tracking. These are not presented as missing or as new inventions.
- Scope the website first, keep current ordering while validating the next system, and choose incremental automation from observed bottlenecks. All consequential actions require appropriate staff/customer approval.

## Excluded from client proposal
Research procurement figures and named customers, unapproved third-party marks, conflicting founding dates, owner/headcount assumptions, market-growth forecasts, assumed ROI, old cost ranges and time windows. None is needed to explain the website direction. No competitor inquiries or client communications were sent.
