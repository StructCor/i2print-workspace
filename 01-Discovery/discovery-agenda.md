## 0. Open (3 min)
- Thank them. Explain the goal: "Today is mostly me listening. I'll show a quick concept at the end."
- Ask permission to take notes (and to record, if you want to; don't record without a clear yes).

## 1. The business (10 min)
- How did i2Print start? How long at 38-19 24th St? *(The site says founded 2000; other sources say 1990.)*
- Who are your best customers? What split between walk-in/local businesses, online orders shipped elsewhere, and resellers/designers?
- Your top 3–5 products by revenue? By margin?
- What do you do better than Vistaprint or 4OVER4? What do customers thank you for?
- Who's on the team, and who handles orders, files and customer emails?

## 2. Goals (8 min)
- If the new website works, what's different in 6 months? (More orders? Fewer phone calls? Less file fixing? Bigger B2B accounts?)
- How do you measure that today? (Orders/month, revenue, quote requests, walk-ins)
- Any deadline? (Season, event, lease, contract renewal)

## 3. Current platform: OnPrintShop (8 min)
- Confirm: the site runs on OnPrintShop? Which plan? Monthly cost? Contract end date?
- What do you like about it? What frustrates you or your customers?
- Who updates products, prices and banners today?
- Do customers have to create an account? Do you see people drop off at that step?
- **Decision prompt:** keep OnPrintShop and fix the front end (Path A), or replace or rebuild (Path B)?

## 4. Files and the "AI fixer" idea (10 min)
- How often do customer files have problems? (Rough % or per day)
- The most common problems: wrong size or shape, low resolution, no bleed, RGB, fonts, text too close to the edge?
- Staff time spent fixing files per week? Do you charge for fixes?
- Your actual specs: bleed per product, safe margin, minimum dpi (small vs. large format), accepted file types, max file size.
- Is there a reprint policy if a customer-approved proof prints wrong?
- **Demo:** show the file-check concept. Ask: "Would this have saved you time this week? What's missing?"
- Comfort with AI extending a background? (It always goes to a proof the customer approves; text and logos are never regenerated.)

## 5. Marketing and email (6 min)
- Newsletter list size? Which tool sends it today? How often?
- Promotions that worked before? Is `NewI2!` (50% off first business cards) a standing offer?
- Google Business Profile: who owns it? Reviews?
- Social: who posts? Is it worth showing jobs coming off the press?
- Interest in automatic emails: welcome offer, "ready for pickup", reorder reminders, seasonal promos?

## 6. Operating facts to confirm (4 min)
- Store hours, and whether pickup is offered.
- Real turnaround by product family, and the order-by cutoff.
- Same-day capability (which products?).
- Photos: is a half-day photo shoot of the shop, presses and team OK?
- Permission to show client logos and client work.
- Vector logo files (AI/SVG/EPS): who has them?

## 7. Budget, timeline, decision (5 min)
- Budget range for the first phase? Monthly comfort for hosting and care?
- Who signs off? Anyone else to include?
- Preferred working style: weekly check-ins, a shared folder, text or email?

## 8. Show and close (5 min)
- Show the homepage concept (2 min) and the file-check demo (2 min).
- Walk through the phased plan and the insurance summary.
- **Next step:** "I'll send a final scope and price for Phase 1 by ____. If it looks right, we start with Discovery & Strategy."
