# i2Print — Discovery Research Plan

**The owner tells you what they want. Their customers show you what will actually work.** This plan adds three small studies around the owner interview, sized for a solo freelancer and a small shop.

## Objectives
1. Learn why customers choose i2Print, and why some don't, in their own words.
2. Find where ordering online breaks down today, from watching real people use i2print.com.
3. Confirm the file-fixer idea solves a real, frequent problem before building it.
4. Agree on one or two measurable success goals with the owner.

## Studies
| # | Study | Who | How many | Time | When |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Owner interview** | Owner / decision-maker | 1 | 60 min | Discovery call (`interview-guide.md`) |
| 2 | **Staff chat** | Whoever handles files and the front counter | 1–2 | 15 min each | Same visit, if the owner agrees |
| 3 | **Usability test of the current site** | Friends/contacts who are small-business owners (not i2Print customers) | 5 | 20 min each | **Before** the call. Gives you evidence to bring |
| 4 | **Customer calls** | i2Print customers, **introduced by the owner** | 3–5 | 15 min each | Week after the call |
| 5 | *(Optional)* 5-question email survey | i2Print's newsletter list, **sent by i2Print** | ~50+ | 1 week | During Phase 1 |

**Ground rules:**
- Never contact i2Print customers directly. The owner introduces you or sends the survey.
- Don't create accounts or place orders on i2print.com yourself, and have test participants stop before checkout.
- Get a clear yes before recording anyone.
- Notes record roles, not names ("office manager, restaurant").

---

## Study 3: Usability test of the current site (do this first)
Five people find most usability problems. Run it over video with screen share, or in person.

**Setup line:** "I'm testing the website, not you. Think out loud. There are no wrong answers. Please stop before you'd enter payment or create an account."

**Tasks:**
1. "You need 250 business cards for your business. Find how much they'd cost and how fast you could get them."
2. "You have a logo file on your computer. Find out whether it'll work for a business card." *(Tests the missing file specs.)*
3. "You need a 6-foot banner for an event next Saturday. Can i2Print do it in time? What would it cost?"
4. "Find out if you can pick up your order in person, and when."
5. "Would you trust this company with your order? Why or why not?"

**Record for each task:** success (yes / partial / no), time, where they hesitated, and their exact words.
**Score:** after each task, ask "How easy was that, from 1 (very hard) to 5 (very easy)?"

## Study 4: Customer calls (15 min, owner-introduced)
1. "How did you first find i2Print?"
2. "What do you usually order? Walk me through the last time."
3. "Have you ordered online with them, or do you call or walk in? Why?"
4. "Have you ever had a problem with a file or a proof? What happened?"
5. "Have you used Vistaprint or other online printers? What's better or worse here?"
6. "If you could change one thing about ordering from i2Print, what would it be?"
7. "Would you open an email from them? What would make it worth opening?"

## Study 5 (optional): 5-question survey
1. How do you usually order from i2Print? (Online · Phone · Email · Walk-in)
2. What do you order most? (multi-select)
3. How easy is ordering online today? (1–5)
4. Have you had trouble getting a file print-ready? (Never · Once · A few times · Often)
5. What one thing would make ordering easier? (open text)

## Synthesis (after the studies)
Put each observation on its own line in `notes-YYYY-MM-DD.md`, then group the lines.

**1. Affinity themes.** Cluster the observations, name each cluster, and count how many sources mention it.

| Theme | Evidence (source · quote / observation) | # sources |
|---|---|---|
| e.g. "Can't tell turnaround" | UT-2: "I have no idea when I'd get these" | 4/5 |

**2. Jobs to be done** (one line each): "When I ___, I want to ___, so I can ___."
- e.g. "When I'm opening a new restaurant, I want menus and signs fast from someone nearby, so I can open on time."

**3. Impact / effort matrix.** Place each fix:

| | Low effort | High effort |
|---|---|---|
| **High impact** | Do first (e.g., fix banner typos, publish file specs and turnaround) | Plan (e.g., file-check tool) |
| **Low impact** | Quick extras | Skip for now |

**4. Highlight reel.** Pick the 5–8 strongest quotes and moments, with role, not name. Use them in the scope proposal ("4 of 5 testers couldn't find turnaround time").

**Deliverable:** `synthesis-report.md`, a one-page summary of themes, the top 5 recommendations, and what changes in scope or price as a result. Send it to the owner with the fixed Phase 1 or Phase 2 quote.
