## Key takeaways for the pitch

1. **The founding-year conflict is settled: 2000.** NY Department of State shows I2 Print Inc. first filed on 2000-02-14. The "1990" on LeadIQ is unsupported. The About page ("Established in 2000") is right.
2. **Tom Leong runs it, with the title in doubt.** LinkedIn says "Manager, i2print" (role since Jan 2000, per search snippet). A press release (not opened) calls him "President". Manta calls him owner. Lindsay's role was **not found** anywhere public.
3. **It is a tiny shop with a strong reputation and almost no marketing reach.** About 3 people (LinkedIn, LeadIQ). Google is 4.7 from 15 reviews. Yelp is 5.0 from 5 recommended reviews, unclaimed, last review July 2025. Instagram has 65 followers, TikTok 13, YouTube 3 subscribers.
4. **Invisible in local search.** i2print did not appear in the Google results for "printing long island city", "business cards LIC queens" or "banner printing queens ny" (checked 2026-10-23). It does own its brand-name search. GSB Digital, 2 blocks away, shows 4.9 from 240 reviews.
5. **The real customers are local, B2B and event/personal work.** Reviews and posts mention restaurant menus, wedding invitations, portfolios, rack cards, CD booklets and an architecture firm. Review count is the gap, not review quality.
6. **No complaints, legal or licensing problems found** in the limited public sources I could reach. Public-record coverage is thin (see section 5), so this is "none found", not "none exist".
7. **Their ordering engine is exposed in small ways.** The Contact page's search title reads "OnPrintShop: Contact Us - i2print", and the Terms page cites both California and New York law. Factual only; nothing here speculates about contracts or developers.
8. **Most pitchable gaps:** local landing pages and schema, a Google review engine, claiming Yelp, hours and map on the site, real shop photos (YouTube already has press and cutting-machine videos they can reuse).

## 6. Outlook (analysis, not fact)

**The local market.** LIC has dense competition for walk-in print: GSB Digital, Cosmos Communications, Influence Print, Masterpiece, Linco, SpeedPro, UPS Store and We Do all appear in Google's results for "printing long island city" (see [competitors.md](competitors.md)). i2print's strongest differentiators are close, hands-on service and a 25-year track record, which its own web presence does not show.

**Area development as customer demand.** Analysis: new residents and businesses create steady demand for menus, moving and event printing, signs and small-business stationery.
- The City Council approved a rezoning for about 14,700 new homes in LIC (https://www.6sqft.com/14700-homes-long-island-city-rezoning-approved, Third-party).
- Court Square and Queens Plaza have large mixed-use projects with ground-floor retail (https://nyrej.com/2026-developing-queens-court-square; https://qns.com/2024/04/queens-plaza-set-for-transformation-with-over-1400-new-luxury-units-by-2026/, Third-party; the second dates from 2024, so timelines may have moved).
- The NY State Comptroller's snapshot of LIC/Sunnyside/Woodside reports business-count growth of 19.5% (2013–2023) versus 15.1% citywide (https://www.osc.ny.gov/files/reports/pdf/report-12-2026.pdf, Third-party).
- I found no current count of LIC businesses or recent openings. New businesses are "possible customers", not a measured pipeline.

**Threats.** Online printers dominate paid search for every query I tried (UPrinting, NextDayFlyers, MOO, Vistaprint, signs.com, FedEx Office). A PRINTING United Alliance survey summary reports commercial printer sales up only 0.3% over the first three quarters of 2025, with most respondents flat or down on profit, and a First Research profile expects consolidation (https://www.printing.org/publication/state-of-the-industry-report-2026, Third-party, summarized through search). I found no data splitting online and local share.

**Opportunities a better website could unlock (analysis).**
1. Rank for "[product] + Long Island City / Queens" with landing pages and local schema. Right now they are not on page 1.
2. Convert strong service into reviews: a post-pickup review request could lift 15 Google reviews toward the 100+ range of local rivals.
3. Show proof they are real and local: shop photos, hours, map, press video, named people. YouTube content already exists.
4. Segment pages for the customers in the reviews: restaurants, weddings/events, architects and designers, small businesses.
5. Capture B2B repeat orders with reorder and quote flows and an email list (needs the real list size).
6. Use the LIC Partnership membership and neighborhood growth as local-credibility hooks (membership needs confirming).
